Kids' Ramadan calendar, digital download

€13.99Digital download

A Ramadan countdown made for one child: their name at the top in big friendly letters, thirty days to colour in, one small picture for every day, and Eid Mubarak at the end.

Choose your design: Amira (night), deep blue with a crescent moon, or Yusuf (dawn), warm and golden. We set your child's name by hand at the top, in English or Arabic, exactly as you write it.

What is included

Print-ready PDFs of your personalised calendar in three sizes, for the Amira and Yusuf designs. Print at home or at any print shop, frame it, and colour in one day at a time through Ramadan. No physical item is shipped for this edition.

How ordering works

Write your child's name and your design choice in the personalisation box on Etsy. We set the name by hand and send you a proof to approve. After your approval you receive the final print-ready files.

Dates and proofreading

The calendar follows Ramadan 1448 in the Umm al-Qura calendar: Monday 8 February to 8 March 2027. Local moon sighting can differ by a day.

Every design begins with our own ideas and creative direction; AI tools assist us in bringing the artwork and mockup images to life.

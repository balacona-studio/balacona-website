Ramadan Planner 1448

Make this Ramadan your most intentional one yet. This planner walks with you from the preparation days in Sha'ban to the last ten nights, Eid and the weeks after, with a dated page for every day of Ramadan 1448, from Monday 8 February 2027.

Every heading is in English with elegant Arabic calligraphy beside it, and the Quran verses and duas are in fully vowelled Arabic with their English translation and source. Made for tablets, and just as good printed at home.

Download starts right after payment. On a tablet, open the PDF in GoodNotes, Notability, Xodo, Samsung Notes or any PDF app; the links and side tabs work right away. On paper, print the whole planner or only the pages you need.

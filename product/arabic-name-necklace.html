Arabic name necklace

€23.77Made to order

Your name, written in Arabic calligraphy and worn close to your heart. This necklace turns a name into the pendant itself: we draw the name in our own Arabic calligraphy, in the flowing Aref Ruqaa style, and our production partner cuts it as one flat nameplate from stainless steel.

A fine link chain attaches at both ends of the name, so it sits centred and balanced on the neck. The sample shown in the photos is the name Maryam, مريم.

LANGUAGE ON THE DESIGN
Arabic + English
Buy on Etsy: Arabic + English

You will open the English listing on Etsy. Enter the name in the personalisation box, in Latin or Arabic letters.

WHAT IS INCLUDED

  • Flat cut-out nameplate in stainless steel, tarnish resistant
  • Finishes: silver colour, gold colour or rose gold colour
  • Stainless steel link chain, 18 inch (45.7 cm) or 20 inch (50.8 cm)
  • Designed by Balacona Studio, made to order by our production partner
Details

The nameplate is cut from stainless steel with a colour finish in silver, gold or rose gold tone. Please allow time for your piece to be made: orders ship within 7 to 10 days, and delivery takes about 3 weeks in total depending on your country. Shipping is charged separately on Etsy.

How ordering works

Place your order on Etsy and enter the name in the personalisation box, in Latin or Arabic letters. We prepare the Arabic calligraphy artwork for your name in our design style, and our production partner cuts your nameplate to order.

Gifting

A personal gift for birthdays, Eid, Ramadan, weddings and new beginnings. Add the recipient's name at checkout and the piece is made for them.

Every design begins with our own ideas and creative direction; AI tools assist us in bringing the artwork and mockup images to life.

Kids' Ramadan calendar, printed

€32.95Printed to order

A Ramadan countdown made for one child: their name at the top in big friendly letters, thirty days to colour in, one small picture for every day, and Eid Mubarak at the end. This edition arrives printed, with a frame if you choose one.

Choose your design: Amira (night), deep blue with a crescent moon, or Yusuf (dawn), warm and golden. We set your child's name by hand at the top, in English or Arabic, exactly as you write it.

LANGUAGE ON THE DESIGN

You will open the English listing on Etsy to choose size and frame. We send a proof before printing.

WHAT IS INCLUDED

  • Your personalised calendar as a printed poster, ready to hang and colour in
  • Sizes 30x40 cm or 50x70 cm
  • Frames: poster only, black, white or natural wood
  • Both designs: Amira (night) or Yusuf (dawn)
  • A proof to approve before printing
What is included

Your personalised calendar as a printed poster, with a frame if you choose one, produced to order and shipped from Europe. Prefer the files only? The digital download edition is a separate listing in our shop.

How ordering works

Choose your size and frame on Etsy, and write your child's name and design choice in the personalisation box. We set the name by hand and send you a proof to approve. After your approval, your calendar is printed and shipped.

Dates and proofreading

The calendar follows Ramadan 1448 in the Umm al-Qura calendar: Monday 8 February to 8 March 2027. Local moon sighting can differ by a day.

Every design begins with our own ideas and creative direction; AI tools assist us in bringing the artwork and mockup images to life.

About Balacona Studio

Balacona Studio is a small design studio in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. We design personalised Arabic calligraphy prints, wedding stationery and bilingual family printables, rooted in the geometry of Moroccan zellige.

Everything we make is sold through our Etsy shop, BalaconaStudio. This website is our showcase and our door for wholesale and creator enquiries; it takes no orders itself.

Contact

For orders, use the Etsy shop, where messages reach us fastest. For wholesale, creators and everything else, write to diasporalifestyleshop@gmail.com.

Until our mailbox on this domain opens, that address is the studio's inbox.

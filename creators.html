Creator collaborations

Create with Balacona Studio.

We collaborate with small creators in family, home, faith and lifestyle content. The model is simple: gifted first, and sharing is always your choice.

What the collaboration looks like

No contracts full of conditions, no scripts. A gift, honestly given.

A product, gifted

Together we choose the design that fits your home or your family, and we send it to you as a gift.

Sharing is optional

If you love it, tell your audience in your own words. A post or a review is always optional and is never a condition of the gift.

Early sight of new designs

You see new designs before they launch, and you tell us honestly what works.

A direct line to the studio

You talk to the studio itself, not to an agency.

How to apply

1

Say hello

Email us your profile links, your country and what you make.

2

Choose together

We suggest the design that fits you best.

3

Receive and decide

The gift arrives. What you share is entirely up to you.

Honest labelling. If you post about a gifted product, please mark it as gifted or as an advertisement, as your platform and your country's rules require. We will never ask you to hide it.

Prefer to be sure first? You can also order a small item yourself; a review of your own purchase on Etsy is then a genuine customer review.
